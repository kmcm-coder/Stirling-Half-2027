Race Morning Protocol

Wake with plenty of time. Eat your familiar carbohydrate-rich breakfast. Sip fluids rather than over-drinking. Arrive early enough for the toilet queue and a calm walk to the start. Start conservatively. If you feel amazing at 5 km, ignore that feeling and keep the effort easy.

When It Doesn't Go To Plan

Tap your current situation for the recovery protocol:

Carry straight on. Do not double up tomorrow. One missed run has zero impact on your 38-week trajectory.
Repeat the previous week instead of progressing. Let your body resume rhythm smoothly.
Recover first. Rest is training. Resuming at 100% health beats grinding at 60% with an aggravating tendon.
